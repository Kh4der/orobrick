03 — Process

From Empty Space to Finished Home

A clear five-step path. No surprises, no mess left behind.

Step 01

Plan the Space

We walk the space with you, measure, listen and map out exactly what your interior needs.

Step 02

Build the Foundation of the Interior

Framing, prep and surface work — the unseen craftsmanship that everything else relies on.

Step 03

Install Floors, Walls, Doors & Windows

The shell of every finished room comes together — square, sealed and beautifully aligned.

Step 04

Finish Cabinets, Bathrooms & Ceilings

The rooms that define daily life are built, tiled, painted and detailed to perfection.

Step 05

Final Touches & Clean Delivery

Trim, molding, polish and a spotless handoff. You walk into a home that’s ready to live in.
